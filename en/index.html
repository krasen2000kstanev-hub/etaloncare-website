DOUG SCHOONCHEMIST · AUTHOR · EDUCATOR

SCIENCE, EXPLAINED FIRST-HAND

Doug Schoon

Doug Schoon's name is known to nail professionals around the world. He is an internationally recognised expert in the science, chemistry and professional products of the nail industry, and a research scientist with a master's degree in chemistry from the University of California.

His approach is built on scientific data, chemistry and a deep understanding of nail structure and how it interacts with professional products. He is a leading authority in the field, known for technical and regulatory work that has helped shape the modern cosmetics industry, and for his work as a scientific expert witness in cases involving cosmetics and products.

His work aims to explain complex scientific processes to professionals in a way that directly matters to their daily practice. As an author he has also been a principal contributor to textbooks, and his expertise extends to several medical reference works.

Over his career he has developed some of the most innovative products in the nail industry. In 1986 he founded Chemical Awareness Training Service (CATS), the first company in the beauty industry specialising in safety training. He then published his acclaimed book Nail Structure & Product Chemistry and the three-part series Face-to-Face with Doug Schoon, which became essential reading for nail specialists. In 2026 he published his brand-new book Advanced Chemistry of Nails, also available in Bulgarian, a modern educational guide to products and nail structure.
