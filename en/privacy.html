Registration and personal data

About the event

“The Science of Nails” with Doug Schoon, 21–22 March 2027, Inter Expo Center, Vitosha Hall, Sofia. Organiser: ETALON CARE. Standard: 650 €. Premium: 750 €. Registration from 1 November 2026.

Choosing and confirming a seat

Your seat is held for 14 days after your request. Payment is by bank transfer: the full amount or a 50% deposit within that period, and the second instalment of 50% by 31 January 2027. Without payment on time, the request expires and the seat is released. If you withdraw or the second instalment is not paid, the seat is lost and the deposit is not refunded.

Information needed for a request

When registration is open we collect your name, email, phone, chosen seat and, if you ask for an invoice, your company details, which are needed to organise your participation. Participants' contact details are not shown on the public seat plan. We send payment details, confirmations and payment reminders to the email you provide.

Data controller

Purpose, legal basis and retention

We use your data to reserve your seat, send payment details, confirmations and reminders, and organise your participation. The legal basis is the performance of a contract. We keep the data for one year from the date of your request, unless the law requires a longer period, for example for accounting documents.

Recipients

The data is stored with Amazon Web Services in the European Union (Frankfurt), and emails are sent through SuperHosting's mail service. We do not sell the data or use it for advertising.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You can exercise them at info@etaloncare.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.
