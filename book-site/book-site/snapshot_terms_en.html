About the book

From the formula to the nail — everything a professional needs to know.

This guide distills over three decades of Doug Schoon’s lab work, publications and consulting into a single accessible yet rigorous book. It explains what actually happens beneath the surface — from monomer molecules to the microstructure of the nail plate.

Written in clear, practical language, it dispels the myths that circulate around salons and delivers the scientifically grounded answers the industry needs.

About the author

Doug Schoon

Research scientist · Author · Forensic expert

Doug Schoon lives in Dana Point, California, and is an internationally recognized scientist holding a Master’s degree in chemistry from UC Irvine. With over 35 years in the cosmetics and personal-care industry, he is a leading authority and forensic scientific expert in cosmetics-related cases.

Founder of Chemical Awareness Training Service (CATS) — the first company in the beauty industry dedicated to safety training. Author of Nail Structure & Product Chemistry, the Face-to-Face with Doug Schoon series, and lead contributor to the Milady and Pivot Point textbooks.
